Research

Our work spans two levels: the intelligent systems that perceive and drive, and the networks that connect them.

System-level research

Making connected vehicles, drones and roadside units perceive and act together.

Multimodal Physical AI

Vision-language models (VLMs), multimodal sensor fusion, and multi-agent learning across connected vehicles, drones, and roadside infrastructure.

Automated Cooperative Driving

ETSI cooperative awareness and collective perception messages (CPM) for intersection negotiation and manoeuvre coordination.

Network Mobility Digital Twin

Coupled mobility-network digital twins integrating traffic microsimulation, sensor models, vehicle dynamics, and wireless communication.

Network-level research

Making the wireless links reliable enough for safety-critical driving.

5G NR-V2X & ITS-G5

Cross-layer resource allocation, MAC scheduling, and congestion control.

6G Access

Integrated Sensing & Communication (ISAC) and reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS).

Cloud–Edge Intelligence

Edge-assisted multi-vehicle sensor fusion, cooperative inference, and mobility-aware task offloading under strict latency bounds.

Featured hardware

JetRacer: Autonomous V2V Swarm

Resource-optimized vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) collision avoidance on dual NVIDIA Jetson Nano modules, with KCF visual tracking and UDP telemetry.
