Connected Automated Systems & V2X Lab

Nestled within the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (Bharti School & CART), our lab integrates Artificial Intelligence, Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communications, and Digital Twins across system and network levels to advance connected, automated mobility and future intelligent transportation.
