ASSISTANT PROFESSOR · IIT DELHI

Dr. Gokulnath Thandavarayan

I am an Assistant Professor at the Centre for Automotive Research and Tribology (CART), and Associate Faculty at the Bharti School of Telecommunication Technology and Management and the Yardi School of Artificial Intelligence (ScAI) at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi.

I received my Ph.D. (Cum Laude) in Wireless Communications and Connected Automated Vehicles (CAVs) from Miguel Hernández University, Spain. My research group collaborates with international academic and automotive industry partners to develop safer, scalable, and intelligent transportation systems for future mobility.
