Dr. Gokulnath Thandavarayan

Assistant Professor at the Centre for Automotive Research and Tribology (CART), and Associate Faculty at the Bharti School of Telecommunication Technology and Management and the Yardi School of Artificial Intelligence (ScAI), Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi.

Ph.D. (Cum Laude) in Wireless Communications and Connected Automated Vehicles (CAVs) from Miguel Hernández University, Spain. The research group works with international academic and automotive industry partners to develop safer, scalable, and intelligent transportation systems for future mobility.
